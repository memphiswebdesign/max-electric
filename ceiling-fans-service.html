Ceiling Fans

Although ceiling fan installation seems straight-forward, there are many factors involved that will help maximize the utility and safety of your ceiling fan. Our expert electrical contractors at Max Electric Inc. know the installation measurements for making your fan highly efficient.

Installing Ceiling Fans In Your Mid South Home. when someone purchases a new home or condo, the first thing they’ll do is to install ceiling fans in each room. This is a good move for more than one reason.

First of all, you’ll save on your utility bill if you use ceiling fans. Often, in hot weather, just the circulation of air can increase the perception of coolness in the room. But also, remember that hot air rises. By turning on the ceiling fan, it will push the warm air down, pull the cool air up, and create a better balance of temperature ranges in the room. With this equalization, you won’t run your air conditioner as much.
